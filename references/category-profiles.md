# Category Profiles

Apply exactly one profile selected at task start. The common technical and relationship rules in `selection-rubric.md` always remain active. For `mixed`, assign each item its dominant story function and apply only that profile to that item. Automatic photo/video pools are fixed by media kind; each profile's reference emphasis guides search terms and the single optional manual recommendation.

## `mixed` — 综合或混合素材

- Route each photo, short clip, or long-video interval to one profile below; do not average every profile together.
- Build a balanced set across place, people, action, detail, transition, and emotional payoff.
- When one item serves two roles, use its dominant role and mention the secondary value in the reason.

## `landscape-travel` — 风景与旅行

- Prioritize atmosphere, light or weather, depth, viewpoint, sense of place, and visual progression.
- Keep a useful sequence: establishing view, movement or arrival, human-scale context, characteristic detail, and payoff.
- Deprioritize generic vistas and repeated angles unless light, action, scale, or story changes.
- Reference emphasis: search the fixed automatic pools for atmosphere, human scale, arrival, and place progression. If requested, recommend one of Unsplash/Pexels for challenge enhancement or Instagram/Xiaohongshu/YouTube/Bilibili for login enhancement according to photo/video and language needs.

## `architecture-space` — 建筑与空间

- Prioritize geometry, vertical control, spatial legibility, material, scale, circulation, and how people use the space.
- Keep exterior context, threshold, wide spatial view, human-scale interaction, and material/detail coverage when available.
- Deprioritize tilted or repetitive frames that add no spatial information.
- Reference emphasis: search the fixed automatic pools for exterior-to-interior progression, scale, material, and circulation. If requested, recommend Unsplash/Pexels for challenge enhancement or Instagram/YouTube/Bilibili for login enhancement according to media kind and language.

## `documentary-culture` — 人文、街头与纪实

- Prioritize authentic action, environmental context, gesture, timing, cultural specificity, and human dignity.
- Prefer scenes that reveal a relationship or lived practice over decorative exoticism.
- Do not let polish override truth, privacy, safety, or a subject's vulnerable context.
- Reference emphasis: prioritize Wikimedia Commons and Flickr evidence within the fixed automatic pools, then use Google discovery for lived practice and context. If requested, recommend one of Pexels, Xiaohongshu, Instagram, YouTube, or Bilibili according to media kind and language.

## `portrait` — 人像与写真

- Prioritize expression, gaze, gesture, identity, flattering light, skin-tone integrity, focus where intended, and background control.
- Keep technically imperfect frames when expression or presence is notably stronger.
- For pose variants, select the strongest expression and place all other usable variants in user review.
- Reference emphasis: search the fixed automatic pools for expression, light, gesture, identity, and background control. If requested, recommend Unsplash or Pexels for challenge enhancement, or Instagram/Xiaohongshu for login enhancement.

## `family` — 家庭与亲情

- Prioritize relationship clarity, emotional truth, family rhythm, voice, ritual, and irreplaceability before polish.
- Treat touch, care, shared attention, reunion, farewell, generations, childhood change, and ordinary daily intimacy as strong evidence.
- Apply the relaxed technical boundary and memory channel exactly; recognizable shaky footage remains reviewable.
- Reference emphasis: search the fixed automatic pools for touch, shared attention, ritual, voice, and family progression. If requested, recommend one of Pexels, Xiaohongshu, Instagram, YouTube, or Bilibili according to media kind and language.

## `friends` — 朋友与友情

- Prioritize shared action, mutual reaction, humor, ease, group chemistry, support, and changes in the relationship.
- Keep candid transitions and reactions around a posed group photo, not only the pose itself.
- Avoid selecting a set dominated by one person when the story is the group, unless the brief says otherwise.
- Reference emphasis: search the fixed automatic pools for shared action, mutual reaction, group chemistry, and progression. If requested, recommend one of Pexels, Xiaohongshu, Instagram, YouTube, or Bilibili according to media kind and language.

## `vlog-event` — Vlog 与活动记录

- Prioritize narrative role, chronological or emotional progression, shot variety, continuity, usable sound, and editability.
- Seek establishing, action, interaction, reaction, detail, transition, and payoff beats instead of scoring isolated beauty alone.
- Judge long videos interval by interval; record precise timecodes and keep the whole source once in timecode mode.
- Reference emphasis: search the fixed automatic video pool for establishing, action, reaction, transition, sound-aware pacing, and payoff; use the fixed photo pool for supporting stills. If requested, recommend Pexels for challenge enhancement or YouTube/Bilibili/Xiaohongshu/Instagram for login enhancement.

## `custom` — 自定义

- Ask for the intended deliverable, audience, must-keep moments, and one to three positive or negative references.
- Convert the answer into three to five explicit keep criteria and three to five exclusion criteria before inspection.
- The common relationship/memory channel remains active unless the user explicitly confirms the collection contains no personal relationship material.
